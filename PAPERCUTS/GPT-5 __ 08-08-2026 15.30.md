# Papercuts

- `rg -o` over multiple Gradle XML result files prefixes each match with its filename, so splitting the output only on `=` and `"` silently produced blank aggregate counts; parse the `<testsuite>` lines directly or suppress filenames before aggregation.
- Back-to-back ADB `input tap` injections were not consistently recognized as Android double tap; issuing both taps in one remote shell with an explicit `100 ms` inter-tap interval produced the accepted detector gesture reproducibly.
