## Confirmed material finding

### F-01 — Samsung/custom-ROM readability and neon-rendering verification debt

- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Evidence:** W26 sizes the idle clock from measured available geometry in
  `DisplayCapability.kt:82-105,535-613,1407-1427` and adds the custom
  `Canvas`/`LinearGradient` circular border path in
  `DisplayCapability.kt:1852-1895`. Host evidence records `188.75` and
  `139.75` clock sizes, transparent `200x200`/`160x160` controls and the
  platform-default gradient interpolation in
  `.tasks/TASK-029-T3-FT-001-W26/geometry.json:32-75`.
  `.tasks/TASK-029-T3-FT-001-W26/target-device.md:3-10` records that no
  authorized Samsung GT-I9300I (`s3ve3gds`) Android 11 custom-ROM observation
  was available; actual target rendering of the adaptive clock and gradient
  circles therefore remains `DEFERRED`. The task card retains the same
  residual risk at `:79-80`, and the final VERIFY retains `TARGET_DEVICE=DEFERRED`
  at `.tasks/TASK-029-T3-FT-001-W26/TASK-029-T3-FT-001-W26-S-VERIFY-final-report-docs-01.md:44-48`.
- **Impact:** The deterministic 1280x720/1024x600 model, contact sheet and host
  tests prove the intended geometry and source-level presentation, but do not
  establish release-target glyph readability or the actual Android rendering
  of transparent neon borders on the Samsung custom ROM. Subsequent idle
  display visual changes therefore still need target observation to establish
  release behavior at the changed rendering boundary.
- **Smallest remediation direction:** When the already-registered target route
  is authorized and available, observe this changed idle Main Display surface
  on the Samsung at 1280x720 landscape: clock readability/no overlap and the
  three transparent, color-distinct neon circles. Record the result without
  promoting host or generic-emulator evidence to Samsung runtime `PASS`; no
  renderer redesign or new product decision is implied.
