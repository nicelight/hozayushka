### F-01 — HIGH — Canonical Samsung/custom-ROM visual verification debt

- **Evidence:** Physical RED/GREEN and independent verifier observation use
  only unlocked TECNO LI6 `1156725456009666` at `2460×1080`; clock bounds are
  `650×201 → 725×218`, and largest illustration is `71×70 → 45×43`
  (`.tasks/TASK-034-T3-FT-001-W31/physical-visual-receipt.md`,
  `.tasks/TASK-034-T3-FT-001-W31/geometry.json`,
  `.tasks/TASK-034-T3-FT-001-W31/verifier-owned-evidence.md`). Host geometry
  at `2460×1080` and `1280×720` is explicitly supporting-only
  (`.tasks/TASK-034-T3-FT-001-W31/host-gates.md`,
  `.tasks/TASK-034-T3-FT-001-W31/TASK-034-T3-FT-001-W31-S-MB-SYNC-final-report-docs-01.md:24-27`).
  The W31 card retains other resolutions/devices and custom-ROM rendering as
  residual risk (`.memory-bank/tasks/TASK-034-T3-FT-001-W31.task.json:92-94`);
  the canonical target is Samsung GT-I9300I Android 11 custom ROM at
  `1280×720` (`.memory-bank/contracts/platform-runtime.md:11-15`,
  `.memory-bank/testing/runtime-verification.md:117-125`).
- **Impact:** The new adaptive clock/card geometry is physically evidenced on
  TECNO, but actual glyph rasterization, clipping, fullscreen composition and
  readability on the canonical custom ROM and other physical resolutions are
  still unobserved. No target-side failure is proven.
- **Owner / next route:** Main Display/runtime-readiness owner and the
  target-device verification owner; use the existing authorized target-device
  route on Samsung `1280×720`, preserving host-versus-device verdict
  separation. This is non-blocking under the runtime contract.
