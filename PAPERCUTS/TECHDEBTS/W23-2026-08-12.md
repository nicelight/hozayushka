## Confirmed material findings

### F-01 — Samsung/custom-ROM display compatibility verification debt (HIGH)

**Evidence.** W21 closes the fixed Main Display geometry while explicitly
recording Samsung/custom-ROM 1280×720 readability, fullscreen and keep-screen-on
as deferred ([W21 card](../../.memory-bank/tasks/TASK-024-T3-FT-001-W21.task.json:42),
[W21 closure record](../../.memory-bank/tasks/TASK-024-T3-FT-001-W21.task.json:53)).
W22 adds the Canvas/Path/Paint illustration layer and independently records
target Canvas compatibility and the same display conditions as `DEFERRED`
([W22 card](../../.memory-bank/tasks/TASK-025-T3-FT-002-W22.task.json:48),
[W22 verification](../../.tasks/TASK-025-T3-FT-002-W22/TASK-025-T3-FT-002-W22-S-VERIFY-final-report-docs-01.md:42)).

**Impact.** Host geometry/contact-sheet and unit evidence do not establish
readability, system-bar/fullscreen behavior, keep-screen-on behavior, or Canvas
rendering compatibility on the release Samsung GT-I9300I Android 11 custom ROM.

**Smallest remediation direction.** When the existing authorized target route
is available, observe the changed display surface on that target and record
the result; do not promote generic-host or other-device output to target PASS.

### F-02 — Physical overdue-audio compatibility verification debt (HIGH)

**Evidence.** W23 host evidence proves request/start/repeat/stop and six
denial/error cases, while its explicit split remains
`HOST_FAKE_RESULT=PASS` and `PHYSICAL_AUDIBILITY=DEFERRED`
([W23 verification](../../.tasks/TASK-026-T3-FT-007-W23/TASK-026-T3-FT-007-W23-S-VERIFY-final-report-docs-01.md:33),
[audibility record](../../.tasks/TASK-026-T3-FT-007-W23/physical-audibility.md:12)).
The changed runtime seam constructs and starts `ToneGenerator` in
`PlatformRuntimeAdapter.kt:123-239`; no authorized target/custom-ROM audible
observation was made.

**Impact.** Fake-platform start and policy matrices do not establish that the
accepted signal is audible at completion, repeats, stops on dismissal/cap, or
behaves as expected under the target's ringer, DND and output-route behavior.

**Smallest remediation direction.** When an authorized Android target is
available, run the existing physical-audibility observation route and record
completion/repeat/dismissal plus ringer/DND/route outcomes. No new audio
framework, permission, scheduler, or product decision is implied.
