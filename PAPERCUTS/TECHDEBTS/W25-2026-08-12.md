## Confirmed material findings

### F-01 — Samsung/custom-ROM Canvas and visual-readability verification debt (HIGH)

**Evidence.** W25 replaces Main Display Unicode pressure `TextView` glyphs with
`PressureArrowView` Canvas/Path shaft and head segments and scales the six
existing illustrations in
`DisplayCapability.kt:150-196,382-435,1707-1745`. The host evidence measures
the reduced envelopes, `1.1789474x` CLEAR disk and 5 px arrows at the accepted
223×444/279×444 geometry, but
[`target-device.md`](../../.tasks/TASK-028-T3-FT-002-W25/target-device.md:11-20)
records no authorized Samsung GT-I9300I (`s3ve3gds`) Android 11 custom-ROM
1280×720 observation. The final functional and semantic reports explicitly
retain `DEFERRED` and do not promote host/contact-sheet evidence to runtime
`PASS` ([VERIFY](../../.tasks/TASK-028-T3-FT-002-W25/TASK-028-T3-FT-002-W25-S-VERIFY-final-report-docs-01.md:23),
[RED-VERIFY](../../.tasks/TASK-028-T3-FT-002-W25/TASK-028-T3-FT-002-W25-S-RED-VERIFY-final-report-docs-01.md:11)).

**Impact.** The evidence does not establish on the release target that the
Canvas renderer is compatible, that the reduced six-state illustrations remain
readable, or that the new UP/DOWN one/two-arrow variants and zero-arrow case
remain visibly distinct without clipping/overlap. This is an evidence debt,
not evidence that the target currently fails.

**Smallest bounded follow-up.** When the already-authorized target route is
available, observe this changed Main Display surface on the Samsung target at
1280×720 landscape and record Canvas rendering, icon readability, arrow
direction/count visibility, zero-arrow absence, clipping and content
non-overlap. Do not promote host or generic-emulator output to Samsung runtime
`PASS`; no new task, dependency, renderer or product decision is implied.
